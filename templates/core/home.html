About Us

Bus Route Finder

Bus Route Finder is a web-based platform designed to help passengers easily find suitable bus routes between their source and destination.

The system provides useful information about buses, bus stops, routes, approximate timings and fares, making public transportation easier to understand and use.

Whether you are a student, daily commuter or travelling to an unfamiliar place, Bus Route Finder helps you find the information you need before starting your journey.

Our goal is to make public bus information simple, accessible and convenient for everyone.
